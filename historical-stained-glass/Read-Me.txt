HERITAGE IN GLASS
200 Historic Cities & Landmarks — Stained-Glass-Style Coaster Art

YOUR FILES
200 individually generated JPG designs, numbered 001–200.
Each exported JPG is 3000 × 3000 pixels, RGB/sRGB, with 300 DPI metadata.
The circular artwork is flattened onto a warm ivory square background. JPG files are NOT transparent. No PNG, SVG, editable layers or machine cutting paths are included.
Also included: Design Index PDF, Design List CSV and these use instructions.

RESOLUTION DISCLOSURE
The artwork was created using generative AI at the original dimensions listed in Design-List.csv and digitally resized to the 3000 × 3000 JPG export dimensions. Export pixel dimensions and DPI metadata do not increase the original captured detail. These are raster illustrations, not vector artwork.

HOW TO USE
1. Download all ten numbered archive parts and extract the ZIP files.
2. Choose a design using the numbered Design Index and matching filenames.
3. Import the JPG into your preferred print-layout software.
4. Match the outer circular artwork to your blank or template. The square canvas includes a narrow ivory margin; its width is not the exact diameter of the circle.
5. Check size, bleed, alignment and color settings for your printer, transfer process and blank supplier.
6. Print a test on ordinary paper before using your final materials. Mirror only when your chosen transfer process requires it.

PRINTING NOTES
Colors vary between screens, printers, inks, coatings and substrates. Follow your equipment and blank manufacturer's settings. No physical sublimation test was performed during file preparation. No universal compatibility with every machine or material is promised. A 300 DPI metadata tag is not a fixed physical coaster size.

ABOUT THE ARTWORK
Original AI-generated decorative interpretations of historical architecture, prepared as a coordinated collection. Buildings may be simplified or stylized; these images are not exact surveys, documentary photographs or historically verified reconstructions. The art is designed in a stained-glass style: no physical glass, finished coasters, equipment or supplies are included. No affiliation with the depicted sites is implied.
